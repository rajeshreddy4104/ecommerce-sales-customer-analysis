# Time-Series & Regional Analysis

## Time-Series Analysis

| Analysis | Description | Business Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly sales trend | Tracks sales and profit by month | Reveals short-term growth and decline patterns |
| Yearly trend | Compares sales, profit, orders, and margin by year | Shows long-term business performance |
| 3-month moving average | Smooths monthly sales volatility | Highlights underlying sales direction |
| Seasonality | Aggregates sales by calendar month | Identifies recurring demand patterns |
| Peak sales periods | Ranks highest-sales months | Supports campaign, inventory, and staffing planning |

## Regional Analysis

| Analysis | Description | Business Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Region-wise profit | Compares profit and margin by region | Identifies profitable regional markets |
| State-wise sales | Ranks states by sales and profit | Finds high-value geographic areas |
| City performance | Ranks cities by sales, profit, orders, and margin | Supports local targeting decisions |
| Regional growth trends | Measures yearly growth by region | Shows where momentum is increasing or slowing |

## Business Interpretation

Time-series analysis explains when performance changes, while regional analysis explains where performance changes. Together, they help leaders decide when to invest, where to expand, and which markets need margin improvement.
