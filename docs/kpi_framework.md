# KPI Framework

This project tracks a core set of business KPIs for e-commerce performance.

| KPI | Definition | Business Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total Sales | Sum of sales revenue | Measures overall revenue performance |
| Total Profit | Sum of profit | Measures business profitability |
| Total Orders | Count of distinct order IDs | Measures order volume |
| Average Order Value | Total sales divided by total orders | Measures average revenue per order |
| Profit Margin % | Total profit divided by total sales | Measures profitability efficiency |
| Top Region | Region with the highest sales | Identifies strongest regional market |
| Top Category | Category with the highest sales | Identifies strongest product category |
| Repeat Customer Rate | Customers with more than one order divided by total customers | Measures customer retention behavior |
