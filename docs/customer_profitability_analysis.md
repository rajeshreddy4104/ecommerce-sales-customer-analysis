# Customer & Profitability Analysis

## Customer Analytics

This project expands customer analysis beyond basic segment totals.

| Analysis | Description | Business Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top customers | Ranks customers by total sales and profit | Identifies high-value customers |
| Repeat customers | Flags customers with more than one distinct order | Measures retention behavior |
| Basic CLV | Uses total customer sales as a simple lifetime value proxy | Estimates customer value over the dataset period |
| Segment analysis | Compares Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office groups | Supports targeted marketing |
| Sales by customer type | Compares repeat customers vs one-time customers | Shows retention contribution to revenue |

## Profitability Analysis

The project also shifts from sales-only reporting to margin-focused analysis.

| Analysis | Description | Business Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profit by category | Compares category-level sales, profit, and margin | Identifies profitable product groups |
| Loss-making products | Lists products with negative total profit | Finds products needing review |
| Discount vs profit | Compares profit margin across discount bands | Helps set discount guardrails |
| Low-margin products | Finds products with weak profit margin | Supports pricing and product strategy |
| Profit contribution | Measures each category/sub-category contribution to total profit | Prioritizes products that drive actual business value |

## Business Interpretation

Sales growth is only valuable when it produces sustainable profit. Customer analytics identifies where revenue comes from, while profitability analysis shows whether that revenue improves business value. Together, these views help decide which customers, products, and discount strategies deserve investment.
